I am a Postdoctoral Associate in [Elizabeth Barnes' group](https://barnes-research.com/) at Boston University. My background includes an undergraduate degree in Physics, a Masters in Civil and Environmental Engineering, and five years in environmental consulting before pursuing a PhD at CU Boulder. I completed my PhD at CU Boulder in Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences, where I worked on machine learning methods for data assimilation and probabilistic emulation of radiative transfer advised by Aneesh Subramanian.

My current research explores the multivariate structure of probabilistic AI/ML forecasts, with applications to forecasting of coastal extremes, S2S prediction, and improving how observations are incorporated into next-generation forecast systems.

See my [CV](/files/cv.pdf), [Publications](/publications/), and [Projects](/projects/).
